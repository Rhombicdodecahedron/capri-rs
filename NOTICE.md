# Notices and credits

## Inspiration

capri-rs is a ground-up Rust rewrite **inspired by
[capri-ai-desktop](https://github.com/edgerunners-2/capri-ai-desktop)** by yume_mud (Capri-cmd),
released under the MIT license. The idea, the character concept and the sprite-sheet format
(clip names, frame counts and sizes, the seat anchor of seated frames) come from that project, so its
skin folders load here unchanged. The code here is new.
