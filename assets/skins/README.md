# Skins

One folder per skin. The folder name is the skin id used in `capri.toml` (`[desk] skin = "..."`).

```
assets/skins/<id>/
  skin.toml          optional: name and credits
  idle.png           required
  blink.png  blink_glitch.png  grin.png  glitch.png  dangle.png  sleep.png  wake.png  mouth.png
  perch.png  perch_blink.png  perch_blink_glitch.png  perch_glitch.png
  lay_awake.png  lay.png  lay_talk.png  lay_talk2.png          optional: lying down
  mini/idle.png  mini/blink.png  mini/talk.png  mini/talk2.png  optional: her mini form
  mini/annoyed.png  mini/annoyed_talk.png  mini/look_left.png  mini/look_right.png  mini/glitch.png
```

Every PNG is a horizontal strip of equally wide frames. The format is the same as the original
capri-ai-desktop (`assets/desk/skins/<id>/`), so those folders can be copied in as they are:

| clip | frames | frame size in the original art |
|---|---|---|
| idle, grin, sleep | 16 | 240 x 384 |
| blink, blink_glitch | 4 | 240 x 384 |
| glitch | 10 | 240 x 384 |
| dangle | 24 | 240 x 384 |
| wake | 13 | 240 x 384 |
| mouth (drawn over the standing face while she talks) | 5 | 240 x 384 |
| perch | 16 | 384 x 384 |
| perch_blink, perch_blink_glitch | 4 | 384 x 384 |
| perch_glitch | 10 | 384 x 384 |

Any height works, frames are scaled to her size. Only `idle.png` is required: a missing clip falls
back to the closest one (`perch_blink` to `perch`, then `idle`).

Standing frames are anchored on her feet (found automatically from the lowest opaque pixels).
Seated frames are anchored on her seat at 30% of the width and 71% of the height, like the original.

### Lying down and mini

These are single pictures, not strips, and any canvas size works: each family is cropped to the area
its pictures draw on together (so switching looks never moves her), then scaled.

- **Lying down** (`lay_awake` eyes open, `lay` eyes shut, `lay_talk` and `lay_talk2` mouth open):
  her body ends up about 1.15 times as long as she is tall, resting on where most of it touches the
  ground (a stray hair spike doesn't lift her). Without `lay_awake.png` she never lies down.
- **Mini** (`mini/idle` looking at you, `blink` eyes shut, also used while she naps, `talk`/`talk2`,
  `annoyed`/`annoyed_talk` after three quick clicks, `look_left`/`look_right` toward the pointer,
  `glitch`): a small screen about a third of her size, sitting on its bottom edge. Without
  `mini/idle.png` she naps full size instead.

A missing look borrows the closest one of its family (`lay_talk2` to `lay_talk`, `mini/annoyed` to
`mini/idle`).

## Included skins

- `capri`: Capri's art by xilia_world00, included with permission and **not** under the MIT license.
  It has every look, lying down and mini included.
- `demo`: placeholder art generated by `tools/gen_demo_skin.py`, MIT.

## Adding a skin

1. Copy a folder with the files above to `assets/skins/<name>/`, or to the `skins` folder in your data
   folder (`cargo run -- paths` shows it) to keep it out of the repository.
2. Add a `skin.toml` with its name and credits.
3. Check the anchors with `cargo run -- preview <name>`: feet on the floor line, seat on the edge line.
4. Pick it in the settings panel, under BODY.
